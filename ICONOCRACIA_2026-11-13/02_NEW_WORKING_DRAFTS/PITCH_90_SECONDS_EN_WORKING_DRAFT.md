# 90-second introduction
Working draft for Ana's review. Approximately 180 words; duration must be tested aloud.

My name is Ana Vitória Vanzin Mendes. I am a doctoral researcher in Law at the Federal University of Santa Catarina, working on female allegories and legal authority in Brazil between 1822 and 1922.

Justice, the Republic and the nation often appear as women, but that observation alone does not tell us what an image did within a legal institution. My project asks who chose these figures, how they entered official settings, and what evidence connects them to particular practices of authority.

I work with object dossiers and legal sources, distinguishing representation and legitimation from formal authentication and legal justification. The cases include constitutional imagery, judicial painting and proposals for official designs. An unexecuted proposal is especially useful because recognition by an institution does not necessarily lead to circulation or public use.

I am also examining France–Brazil connections through documented routes, rather than assuming that visual similarity proves influence.

The project is still developing, and I want its conclusions to depend on the sources. I would value a conversation about its contribution to legal history, the most productive cases, and possible settings for further doctoral research.
